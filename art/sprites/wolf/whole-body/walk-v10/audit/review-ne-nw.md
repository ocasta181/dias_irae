## Cycle verdict and repair priorities

1. **Repair whole-body motion for both full cycles.** All 24 poses need a coherent phase progression of shoulder/hip mass, abdomen, neck/head attitude, tail and fur with the changing supports. The current visually held three-frame upper bodies and group-boundary replacements fail the user's requirement. The authoring instruction to keep head/spine identical while only changing limbs conflicts with that outcome. Redraw intact wolves per pose, retain fixed registration and the approved paw plan, and build the motion into the drawings. Do not solve this with separated painted parts or a runtime bob.
2. **Recover visible paw trajectories from the actual painted pixels.** Every row has visible endpoint disagreement or an unverified hidden contact. Prioritize the reaching forepaw landing transitions NE09→10 and NW03→04, where the intended paw barely changes while the painted paw retracts abruptly. Then repair the consistently high fore/hind soles and the hind contacts displaced toward the outer silhouette. Annotate real visible soles and joints; mark hidden contacts rather than copying guide coordinates into measurements.
3. **Rebuild chronological boundaries and seams.** Explicitly compare NE03→04, 06→07, 09→10, 12→01 and NW03→04, 06→07, 09→10, 12→01. Keep the same camera, head volume, ear construction, rump/tail mass and fur markings while adding phase-driven deformation. Current changes at these boundaries are appearance/shape jumps. Preserve stable identity while allowing the entire animal to move.
4. **Restore the original proportion and medium.** The rear diagonals retain grey-brown, rough fur and canine features, but both read as relatively small skulls on long backs/rump masses compared with the huge broad head and tiny squat body of `base-wolf-v02.png`. The original has dense gritty dry-painted texture; these frames read smoother with broad repeated coat bands at this viewing scale. Re-establish those original proportions and markings in the intact rear three-quarter design before extending repairs to all poses. The upper-left/upper-right directions are recognizable; no obvious howling pose was observed.
5. **Verify repairs in exported playback.** After frame geometry and whole-body continuity are repaired, inspect normal and slow playback, reduced game scale, both loop seams and root travel using the exported package. This review supplies frame-specific visual failures, not a contact-budget pass or final animation approval.

Next action: integrate these defects with the other direction reviews, revise the original-reference plus exact-pose authoring control to require coordinated whole-body phase changes, and repair a bounded pilot around a forepaw landing boundary plus its adjacent body phases. Reinspect every affected pair and seam before promoting the repaired cycle.
