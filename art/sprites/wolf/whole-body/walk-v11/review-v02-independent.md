## Independent cycle assessment

V02 is not identical to V01's failure. The early exposed near-front stance retreat in E-01…04 is a visible improvement worth recording. Character identity is fairly stable, every still is a connected furry quadruped, and there is some local chest/thigh contour change rather than literally zero body motion. The supplied views retain the E camera and recognizable original coat/face traits.

The complete support sequence still does not match. Far LF's E-01…03 advance is low or covered; its landing/support cannot be traced confidently through E-04…06. E-07/08 present a front-paw travel/depth conflict, and E-10/11 again put one front paw behind the near-front stance target and the other ahead of the far-front stance target. The near hind lane is often high or obscured. Those are visible geometry and readability defects, not solved by assuming invisible paws sit at their planned coordinates.

The body does move, but that does not satisfy the user's requested motion. Face/ear/ruff/back pattern and tail appear nearly held over ranges. Lower limbs and local attachments change more than the torso/head. The conspicuous change is the upward E-08→09 discontinuity followed by E-09…12 staying high and 12→01 dropping back. That is not a smooth coordinated shoulder/chest roll, haunch rotation, abdominal deformation, neck/head counter-movement and fur/tail response tied to support. The control's small smooth body excursions do not explain this large visible boundary change. Exact magnitude and contact error belong to independent measurements, not this manual inspection.

The overall painted extent still stretches beyond the fresh control at nose and tail and reads longer/more horizontal than the tiny squat volume target. That is secondary to the decisive paw and loop failures. Do not fit or recenter individual frames to hide the height reset; correct the intact drawings and verify registration independently.

## Retry and method verdict

The V02 rerun was justified because V01 supplied contradictory muzzle geometry. It removes a known authoring error from the experiment. It does not establish that this free-form sheet method can reliably satisfy the anatomy, support lanes, whole-body motion or temporal loop.

**Another similar free-form full-cycle retry is not justified now.** The request already explicitly names coordinated whole-body deformation, anatomical near/far lanes, all numerical targets and seam continuity. V02 retains fundamental lane/support and body-height failures. These two outputs are evidence against repeating the same complete-sheet handoff, not proof that the concept itself is impossible.

The next method must prove intact pose geometry and anatomical occlusion on a difficult adjacent pair before producing another full sheet. E-07→08 is suitable: near RF must swing forward with lift while far LF remains planted and retreats, with connected shoulder/chest, neck/head, hip/abdomen and tail response. Use explicit pose conditioning or deliberate manual complete-frame drawing with anatomical full-body silhouettes and differentiated near/far paths. Keep the original identity authority and corrected consistent controls. No painted-part kit, runtime body bob, independent per-frame placement correction or generic replacement walk satisfies the request.

Acceptance requires visible pose correspondence, actual annotated paw/attachment landmarks with occlusion uncertainty, correct support lanes, stable proportions/coat, coordinated body articulation and a continuous 12→01 loop. A method that cannot establish those on the small pair should report its limitation rather than expand the rejected output.

Uncertainty: this is a static chronological visual review, not exact paw/head measurements or runtime playback. Small intended subpixel-scale body changes may be hard to judge at the logical size; I do not claim that every body pixel is frozen. Short furry paws and overlap obscure identities/undersides, so hidden contacts remain unverified. No world-contact, timing, exported playback, start/stop or engine-integration pass is claimed. All twelve V02 frames remain rejected candidates outside the active animation.
