DIAS IRAE · WOLF WALK AUDIT

Exact stick figures for all eight walk frames

These diagrams redraw the existing v03 target coordinates. They are not a new motion plan and were not the images sent to generation. The original guide for poses 1–4 and original guide for poses 5–8 were supplied with the intact wolf base, S13 and G15.

The generated walk does not follow this plan. Four drawings were reordered in playback. The planned 36-pixel stride was not established from measured painted steps. The current walk remains rejected; changing speed did not repair the poses.
