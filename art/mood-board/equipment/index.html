12th-century European equipment.

42 active references: 40 from the 12th century (95.2%) and 2 spanning the early 13th (4.8%). The early-13th allowance is 1201–1230; no active reference extends beyond 1230.

The period rule applies throughout: helmets, torso armor, gloves, leggings, footwear, shields, weapons, clergy, townspeople and nobles. Ten new 12th-century helmet/headpiece comparisons supplement the retained museum helmet. Their different outlines include banded, curled, rounded, pointed, fluted, open and face-protecting forms.

The original mood board remains approved. Equipment choices await your selection. The story remains 1101; Guarin’s full-face greathelm exception and exaggerated proportions remain authoritative. Detail views link to the unchanged full images. Object/design dates and photographic dates are separate.
