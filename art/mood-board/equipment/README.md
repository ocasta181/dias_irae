# 12th-century European equipment board

[Open the interactive gallery](index.html?v=4): **42 active references: 40 from the 12th century (95.2%) and 2 spanning the early 13th (4.8%). The early-13th allowance is 1201–1230; no active reference extends beyond 1230.**

This replaces the previous 12th–14th-century scope in every category. Ten new 12th-century helmet/headpiece views supplement E24. There are 11 headpiece, 3 torso, 2 hand, 3 legwear, 3 footwear, 3 shield, 6 weapon/tool, 3 clerical, 4 town clothing and 4 noble clothing choices. The two early-13th allowances are E60 (cloth under mail, 1200–1225) and E74 (a dagger pommel attributed to late 12th–early 13th, approximately 1180–1230).

Surviving artifacts, period artwork and modern reconstructions are labeled separately. Cropped viewports make small illustrated equipment readable; click for the complete unchanged image. Ten helmet views are not ten independently surviving helmet families. Some sources cannot settle construction; these limits appear on the cards. In particular, the dagger reference is a pommel, the knife is an illustration, the axes are work tools, and the scale cuirass is a Sicilian/Byzantine regional comparison. No later equipment is used to fill those gaps.

Forty excluded sources retain their files, identifiers, provenance and review notes. They are history, not active concept references. The original 26-image mood board remains approved.
