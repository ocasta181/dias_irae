# Dress and equipment review manifest

**36 active dated European references, pending review**, spanning the 12th, 13th and 14th centuries. Eleven earlier entries are excluded from the active selection and retained only as history. No 15th-century style is proposed. The approved original 26-image mood board is unchanged.

Open [the review gallery](index.html). Every active view supports Accept, Reject, Revise, Pending and commentary. Download and save this file as `art/mood-board/equipment/manifest.md`, or give selections in chat. Browser drafts do not edit repository files. Export preserves notes for excluded historical entries as well.

Dates identify the object or depicted/represented style, not the photograph. Approximate and uncertain dates are stated openly. A modern reproduction is labeled as such; period depictions are not surviving equipment. The story remains 1101; Guarin’s full-face greathelm is the allowed exception. Other later equipment is a selection candidate, not automatic story canon. Historical anatomy must not replace our oversized heads, tiny bodies and short limbs.
