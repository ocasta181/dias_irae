# Dress and equipment review manifest

**Equipment board 04 — 42 active references: 40 from the 12th century (95.2%) and 2 spanning the early 13th (4.8%). The early-13th allowance is 1201–1230; no active reference extends beyond 1230.**

The date rule applies to every category. Ten new 12th-century helmet/headpiece comparisons supplement E24. These are visual options, not ten independently surviving helmet families. Forty superseded references remain in history with stable identifiers, files and review notes.

Open [the interactive review gallery](index.html?v=4). Every active card supports Accept, Reject, Revise, Pending and written commentary. Download and save this file as `art/mood-board/equipment/manifest.md`, or give selections in chat. Browser drafts do not modify repository files. Export preserves historical-row decisions and notes.

Dates describe the object, illustration or a modern reconstruction’s represented style, not its photograph. Source qualifiers remain explicit. Manuscript drawings are useful design evidence, not proof of every construction detail or of French clothing in 1101. The original 26-image mood board remains approved. Guarin’s full-face greathelm exception remains allowed; oversized heads, tiny bodies and short limbs remain required. Equipment selections await user approval.
