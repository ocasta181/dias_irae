Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original Guarin style candidate for Dias Irae. The user demands RADICALLY DIFFERENT drawing systems, not the same shaded chibi knight in different outfits.
Subject: one full-body weary adult knight with an oversized helmet/head, tiny body and short limbs, cute in silhouette but solemn in mood. About two-and-a-half head heights. Same ordinary mail, dark wine-red cloth, taupe boots, wooden kite shield and short straight sword. Elevated three-quarter view, simple neutral stance on charcoal ground.
Input image 1: real greathelm SHAPE reference only. Use its flat crown, cylindrical enclosing barrel sides, narrow visor slit and small breathing holes. It is not a rendering/style, brightness, corrosion, museum scene or detail reference. Full face covered; no visible human eyes, nose or mouth. No pointed conical/nasal helmet, no oversized eyeholes. The greathelm is an explicitly accepted anachronism.
Sword geometry: rigid straight center axis from pommel through grip, perpendicular crossguard, straight symmetrical double-edged blade and centered point. No kink at guard, no curved blade/fullers. Angle the whole straight object downward; never bend its geometry.
Art board constraints: Hollow Knight / Castle Crashers compact head/body structure, Diablo II darkness/material weight, rural medieval mail and worn cloth. Dark charcoal, soot taupe, muted oxblood, dull iron. No bright cream helmet or shield, no white facial highlights, no gold/glow, horns, fantasy symbols, copied characters, captions, logo, portraits or UI. Hidden splinter stays hidden.
Critical rendering constraint: do NOT use previous generated character images. Do NOT reproduce the familiar textured cel-shaded chibi rendering. Every medium below is a completely different construction method. Remain 2D, graphic/cartoon with fine pixel-ready edges, never large mosaic pixels, 3D/voxel/sculpted toy, photographic or realistic anatomy.
Unique drawing system: SHADOW CUT-PAPER THEATER. Build the entire character from 8–12 broad flat paper silhouette shapes, with ONLY a thin second tone along selected seams. Absolutely no brush texture, metal scratches, mail loops, crosshatching, gradients, lighting render or dimensional shading. Heavy negative-space graphic design: very large dark barrel-head, tiny tapered pear-shaped body, short flat mittens/boots, one red-brown cloth silhouette. Faceplate is one dark slate rectangle with one narrow black visor. Mail is one charcoal field with at most three simple incised marks. Sword is a perfectly straight geometric grey taper, shield one dark trapezoid/kite. Crisp broad curves interrupted by subtle tiny steps. The character must feel like an austere medieval shadow puppet / paper collage. Keep enough low contrast grey and dark wine to distinguish from background. This must look radically more reductive and planar than either an engraved print or textile embroidery.
