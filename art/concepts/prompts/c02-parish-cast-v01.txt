Asset type: original concept candidate for Dias Irae, a dark pixelated isometric action role-playing game.
Style/medium: dark pixel art concept illustration with crisp stepped edges, deliberate clusters of pixels, shaped shadows and selective dark outlines. Moderate stylization: more cartoony than Diablo II, distinctly less cartoony than Hollow Knight. Human proportions are weighty and believable, around five to six heads tall for adults; faces readable but restrained. Clear silhouettes and simplified masses, with enough clustered material texture to distinguish mail, wool, wood and stone. Historical constraints: late summer 1101, rural France. Mail hauberks, nasal helms, kite shields and ordinary arming swords. Small Romanesque stone buildings, round arches, barrel vaults, limewash and painted apses. No plate armor, enclosed great helms, oversized fantasy swords, Gothic pointed arches, modern objects or copied game characters. Story identities and period equipment override reference-game costumes. No interface, logo or watermark.
Output intent: a reviewable concept image, not a finished sprite sheet, atlas, animation or game screenshot. Keep the elevated three-quarter/isometric game context where relevant.
