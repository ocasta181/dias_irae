HARD MEDIUM RULE: completely FLAT hand-drawn 2D game art. Even the helmet, boots and shield use flat color areas. No 3D or 2.5D appearance, modeled rounded shading, gradients, bevels, realistic reflections, rim light, sculpt, CG miniature or material-render lighting. Dirt consists of flat marks, not lighting that describes volume. Use only two dark values per material. A top plane may have one flat slightly different value to communicate the drawn view; never a rounded highlight.

MOOD: darker, grittier, muddier, dirtier, drearier, hopeless, poor, wretched, worn, tired, desperate. A small returning knight whose gear has survived rain, famine and too much walking: sagging shoulders, head heavy but upright, sword hanging low, shield held wearily. He is endearing only through oversized-head/tiny-body structure, never cheerful, clean, proud, heroic or fashionable. Encrusted mud, greasy soot, abraded iron, threadbare cloth, split wood, frayed wraps and old stains. Dark dried-brown/wine cloth almost merges into charcoal; avoid pink, fresh red and pale silver. Very sparse dull gray marks, no attractive gleam.

APPROVED BOARD: Hollow Knight supplies compact rounded silhouettes, readable 2D shapes and darkness; Diablo II supplies grim earthy colors and wear, NOT its realistic anatomy or rendered depth. Castle Crashers contributes only a trace of squat proportions, no bright color or comedy. Darkwood and the sacred-horror paintings supply isolation, black recesses and faded dried red. The role of these references is mood and drawing structure, not copying characters, symbols or outfits.

FIXED SUBJECT: Guarin, rural household knight in the Royans/Isere story set in 1101. Huge enclosed barrel greathelm, tiny broad torso, stubby arms/legs and small wide boots, approximately two helmet-heights tall. Face and eyes entirely hidden. Helmet is a coherent upright cylinder roughly as tall as wide: level flat lid SAME DIAMETER as the helmet sides, NO brim, overhang or pancake cap. Narrow black visor in a substantial intact faceplate, two plain vertical rivet strips and small breathing holes. Greathelm is the explicit anachronism. Plain dark ring-mail shirt/short sleeves, ragged very faded wine scarf and short cape and waist cloth. Brown belt/plain rectangular buckle, plain brown gloves, muddy taupe-brown forearm/shin cloth wraps, short rounded brown boots. No metal knee/elbow plates, bare skin, jewelry, emblems or new costume pieces. Show mail with economical tiny interlocking broken ring marks, not scales or huge discs.

In viewer-left hand: straight double-edged arming sword, disk pommel, brown grip and straight crossguard, intact straight blade pointing low across the front toward lower-right. At viewer-right: elongated pointed taupe wooden kite shield, dull iron rim, one narrow extremely faded dirty wine-red panel. Same loadout in all styles; wear changes its condition, not its identity.

COMPOSITION: ONE whole character, square canvas, quiet charcoal ground, small flat muddy foot mark, no writing, decorative border or panels. Elevated orthographic three-quarter game view, about 45 degrees above the ground, facing diagonally lower-right. Show the level lid as a broad ellipse aligned with the cylinder's sides and a substantial visible front faceplate below it. Keep the helmet upright and structurally coherent. Body is squat and foreshortened, but both feet, sword and shield tips remain readable. Do not solve the angle by flattening the helmet into an oversized plate.
