The entire frame is a dirty, subdued handmade painting: scumbled opaque gouache, translucent smoky watercolor stains, heavily worn rubbed pigment, visibly irregular broad brush edges, textured slate-grey and peat-brown washes. The knight's little armor is painted with chunky wet and dry strokes, NOT traced in black ink. Everything has the same painterly surface, including the church and muddy ground. Eliminate clean ink outlines and pen hatching. Deep muted graphite-grey, dirty olive-brown, coal and exhausted wine colors. Dark overcast world. No pale paper border or bright ochre ground. Like a game running inside a worn old painting. Cute squat silhouette, heavy distress and tiny scale. The painting is flat on the screen with intentional simple drawn shapes, no realistic lighting or render.

Create one wide 16:9 example gameplay screen for Dias Irae, from scratch. ONLY TWO-DIMENSIONAL drawn art. No 3D, no 2.5D models, no miniature render, no glossy beveled materials. Maintain this rendering medium across the whole frame.

CAMERA IS A GAME CAMERA, zoomed well OUT, looking DOWN at the whole village encounter, around 45 degrees above the ground, orthographic three-quarter projection, no horizon. The entire player figure is small: at most 70 pixels high within a 1280 x 720 frame (including helmet and feet), approximately one tenth of screen height. All neighbors are the same approximate figure size; the knight must not be a giant foreground portrait. Draw his helmet top as a broad ellipse whose short diameter is about 70 percent of its long diameter. Draw the actors with visible crowns and compressed small torsos from above, using the SAME elevated view as the buildings and paths. Tiny ground contacts, clear traversable path. No panoramic horizon, no character showcase, no close-up inserts.

Helmet is half his total height, tiny broad torso, extremely short stubby limbs, squat endearing silhouette. FULLY CLOSED iron cylindrical greathelm, one black eye slit, a few small breathing holes, no exposed eyes, no face, no visible hair. A straight dull steel sword with a simple crossguard in his right hand, a taupe wooden kite shield with one NARROW faded wine-red vertical stripe in his left. No outfit substitutions, no bright plate suit, no adult realistic proportions. Both helmet and body are drawn from above. Greathelm is our explicit deliberate anachronism; architecture remains early medieval.

This is fictional Rivoire in the Royans/Isere setting, southeastern France, late summer 1101. A small modest Romanesque parish church stands upper left: thick limestone walls, simple ROUND arches, tiny rural bell feature, no grand tower or cathedral, no Gothic pointed arches. Low poor limestone/timber houses, blighted rye plots and a muddy central-right well. Upper right: burnt shattered watermill beside a narrow stream, wet black broken timbers and a clotted wheel. Two exhausted recognizable parishioners in simple worn tunics/hose and one gaunt ordinary wolf form a readable encounter ahead of the player. Not armored undead, not demons, not skeleton troops. Clear movement room between the little enemies and scene objects. Same basic church/path/well/mill encounter in this rendering medium, a playable environment rather than backdrop art.
