PRIMARY AND MANDATORY RENDERING MEDIUM:
Use dark hand-drawn opaque textured color blocks contained by thick rough almost-black chipped contours. Tiny scratch marks, carefully grouped ring-mail loops, worn rivet dots, chalky stipple on grey flat helmet surfaces; short firm dirty contour arcs on boots and wraps. Texture is fine, hand-drawn and gritty, like worn drawn game sprites. Avoid the thin tidy pen sketch used for storybook illustrations. Strong thick silhouette contours, controlled graphic internal detail, crisp dark edge against muddy textured ground. NO watercolor pooling, NO smooth paint blends, NO clean sparse comic art. This is the sharper, more deliberately outlined variant of dark drawn fantasy game art. Large helmet on tiny broad body, flat fill shapes only.

Create one wide 16:9 example gameplay screen for Dias Irae, from scratch. ONLY TWO-DIMENSIONAL drawn art. No 3D, no 2.5D models, no miniature render, no glossy beveled materials. Maintain this rendering medium across the whole frame.

CAMERA IS A GAME CAMERA, zoomed well OUT, looking DOWN at the whole village encounter, around 45 degrees above the ground, orthographic three-quarter projection, no horizon. The entire player figure is small: at most 70 pixels high within a 1280 x 720 frame (including helmet and feet), approximately one tenth of screen height. All neighbors are the same approximate figure size; the knight must not be a giant foreground portrait. Draw his helmet top as a broad ellipse whose short diameter is about 70 percent of its long diameter. Draw the actors with visible crowns and compressed small torsos from above, using the SAME elevated view as the buildings and paths. Tiny ground contacts, clear traversable path. No panoramic horizon, no character showcase, no close-up inserts.

The one player character Guarin stands near the lower-middle of the world, facing up-right along an open muddy diagonal path. His whole body is only TWO HELMET-HEIGHTS tall. Helmet is half his total height, tiny broad torso, extremely short stubby limbs, squat endearing silhouette. FULLY CLOSED iron cylindrical greathelm, one black eye slit, a few small breathing holes, no exposed eyes, no face, no visible hair. Simple worn dark ring mail, faded wine-red torn scarf/cape and short waistcloth, brown leather gloves/belt, dirty brown leg wraps and very short brown boots. A straight dull steel sword with a simple crossguard in his right hand, a taupe wooden kite shield with one NARROW faded wine-red vertical stripe in his left. No outfit substitutions, no bright plate suit, no adult realistic proportions. Both helmet and body are drawn from above. Greathelm is our explicit deliberate anachronism; architecture remains early medieval.

This is fictional Rivoire in the Royans/Isere setting, southeastern France, late summer 1101. A small modest Romanesque parish church stands upper left: thick limestone walls, simple ROUND arches, tiny rural bell feature, no grand tower or cathedral, no Gothic pointed arches. Low poor limestone/timber houses, blighted rye plots and a muddy central-right well. Upper right: burnt shattered watermill beside a narrow stream, wet black broken timbers and a clotted wheel. Two exhausted recognizable parishioners in simple worn tunics/hose and one gaunt ordinary wolf form a readable encounter ahead of the player. Not armored undead, not demons, not skeleton troops. Clear movement room between the little enemies and scene objects. Same basic church/path/well/mill encounter in this rendering medium, a playable environment rather than backdrop art.

Dark, worn, poor, dirty, wretched, tired, desperate, dreary and hopeless. Darkwood's unease, Diablo II's ruined rural world and material filth, Hollow Knight's little endearing oversized-head silhouettes and economical shapes. Only a light trace of Castle Crashers squatness. No cheerful sunshine, saturated colors, magical glow, god rays or polished comic action. Do not copy any existing game's character or interface.

A restrained charcoal bottom interface strip occupies about 7 percent of the frame: tiny muted wine and dirty grey-blue resource shapes, four empty small square slots. NO words, numbers, logos, watermarks, ornate golden frame or inventory screen. Most space belongs to the game world.
