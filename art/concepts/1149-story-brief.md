# Active story brief: Robert of Rookmere, autumn 1149

The active setting is [England](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=England), around [Lewes](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lewes%2C+East+Sussex%2C+United+Kingdom), after the Second Crusade. The plot, cast, and locations are in [the outline](../../story/story-arch.md), [character dossiers](../../story/characters.md), and [location brief](../../story/locations.md). The source-grounded decisions and earlier-name correspondences are in [the reconsideration](../../docs/research/1149-england/story-reconsideration.md).

## What the revision changes

**Robert of Rookmere** is the protagonist's active name. **Alice, Hugh, Oswin, Thomas, Wulfstan, Edith, Godric, Anselm, Gilbert, Hamon, Walter, Agatha, and Ernald** belong to the new cast. Their roles are defined by the revised dossiers, not by matching each name to an old image.

Wulfstan is a caregiver and ally; Godric is a living household sergeant; Hugh can survive release and help others. Hamon is a lay estate agent, with a cap and seal case rather than a bishop's mitre. Gilbert leads an invented predatory veterans' fraternity inspired by already existing religious-knightly ideals. His badge is an invention, not a verified order emblem. Edith is a child and witness, without a flagellant costume or public saint title.

The main scene circuit is the fictional Rookmere manor and parish, mill and collection yard, supply escort, House of Saint Michael, market neighborhood and coercive enclosure, veterans' refuge, Blackthorn Hold, and supernatural descent. Use the new location functions and evidence rather than moving the old French gorge, shrine, charnel, and mithraeum across the sea.

## What the existing art records still establish

Selected source images, rendering medium, proportions, face preferences, image IDs, and review decisions retain their recorded history. This writing revision does not choose a new style, source image, camera calibration, or production sprite. Keep earlier files and hashes intact.

Earlier records use the source name **Guarin** and the former cast names. That is provenance. Production-facing labels for the current protagonist use **Robert**; the old name does not reinstate the former biography. Asset paths containing the source name remain stable because tools and existing studies use them.

The existing visual brief records the author's explicit allowance for a **full-face greathelm**. Preserve it as a deliberate visual exception if that approved design is used. Historical research still places enclosed great-helm forms later than 1149; a date change alone does not turn the exception into documented period equipment. Nasal helmets remain the conservative historical alternative. Do not silently replace an exact approved input or revoke its exception.

## Current world and encounter priorities

- A poor, worked countryside with varied crops, grazing, ordinary stores, and one compromised relief route.
- A household and parish where useful work and genuine worship continue.
- A care house the player defends, with patients and caregivers moving out of danger.
- Collectors, guarded supplies, a misused household seal, witnesses, and captives: particular abuses of authority.
- Veterans who can choose protection or predation; injuries do not identify enemy status.
- A damaged local hold reused as a depot, followed by explicitly impossible supernatural depth.
- Revelation's locust imagery for the final hostile presence, alongside a community whose recovery remains visible.

The new research dates real orders and costume references. An Antonine tau uniform, standard leper-knight costume, Teutonic mantle, later plate harness, or complete later town defenses are not automatically part of this setting.
