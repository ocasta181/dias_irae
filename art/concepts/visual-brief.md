## Rendering target

Dark, pixelated, cute-proportioned isometric action role-playing game. Hollow Knight and Castle Crashers guide character structure: large heads, compact bodies and short limbs, with readable expressions and silhouettes. Diablo II remains relevant to material weight, environmental texture, composition and grounded darkness; its realistic human proportions do not govern the new characters. Darkwood supports the unease of ordinary spaces.

People remain recognizably adult or child through clothing, role and restrained facial details, with deliberately oversized heads and tiny bodies. Keep the overall feel cute without large round eyes. Guarin's whole face is enclosed by metal, with narrow visor openings and no visible eyes in this comparison. The selected S09 uses dark textured cartoon shapes with fine pixel-adjacent edges. Preserve the preferred S13/S17 surface finish while explicitly restoring the exaggerated body structure. Future comparisons, if requested, must differ in construction and rendering language rather than outfits. Final rendering follows the reviewed candidate; pixel density, sprite dimensions, animation directions and engine import settings remain for the reviewed pilot.

The game references guide drawing and readability. The story supplies identities, anatomy, equipment, buildings and symbols. The user explicitly confirmed the Diablo II elevated isometric gameplay view; use that camera in character concepts, rather than a side-scroller or near-frontal illustration. The camera attempts remain unapproved. Prompted numeric angles were requests, not certified output geometry. A new measured 3D composition guide is proposed for the next provider pilot; character identity sheets may also include close material details. Production projection, scale and proportions will be fixed after review.

## Story constraints

- Late summer 1101; rural homecoming, diseased rye, household grief and sacramental horror.
- Small Romanesque buildings: round arches, low barrel vaults, slit windows, limewash and painted apses. Equipment: mail, kite shields and arming swords. **User-directed exception for Guarin:** a fully enclosed cylindrical greathelm with a flat crown and narrow visor overrides the earlier open nasal-helm instruction; the user explicitly allows this anachronism. This is a deliberate art-direction choice; do not silently change the story files or treat the concept as historical evidence.
- Palette: rye gold, charcoal, limewash, Antonine black and taupe, dried wine, pig-fat yellow, bone white and dull iron. Local warm light against cold shadow; luminous supernatural accents are rare.
- The Isenheim references govern spiritual intensity and sacred light, not period architecture or costume.
- Act I enemies remain recognizable parishioners and wolves. Raimon remains an elderly household officer inside the changed body. Overt demons and locust armies belong later.
- Guarin is 34, road-worn and tall; a white streak above his left ear, a shortened right little finger, period travel clothes and mail. The rag-wrapped splinter is kept concealed over his sternum.
- Almodis is 28, tall, covered dark hair, faded red-brown wool and a thin wedding band. Odo is 22, underfed, sunburnt tonsure, crooked tau and ink on his left fingers. Giraud is 47, thin, grey alb and scarred palms. Agnes is 11, rye-dust in her hair, unequal pupils and a rope belt.
- Raimon is 63: missing lower legs below the knee, horse-hide bindings, borrowed uncanny supports, household keys on an old belt. His exact changed anatomy is a concept proposal for review, not new story canon.
