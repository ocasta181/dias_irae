# Historical research for Dias Irae: autumn 1149

**This is the active setting dossier.** The campaign is set in [England](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=England), around [Lewes](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lewes%2C+East+Sussex%2C+United+Kingdom), from autumn into early winter **1149**, with an epilogue in **1150**. Robert of Rookmere returns after the failed eastern expedition of the Second Crusade. The date, English backdrop, and fresh story reconsideration were authorized by the author on 6 October 2026.

Rookmere, its parish, the House of Saint Michael, the veterans' fraternity, the local enclosure, Blackthorn Hold, and the supernatural crisis are fiction. The historical town, Warenne succession, religious institutions, and national political frame supply their surroundings. No real family or religious house is identified as secretly responsible for the invented crimes.

## Decisions that matter to the campaign

- **The decisive eastern failure is in July 1148.** Autumn 1149 allows return travel; it does not move the failed siege into another year.
- **Stephen is still king.** The player's local victory does not anticipate the later succession settlement or Henry II's accession.
- **William III de Warenne is dead.** His loss on the same crusade gives household uncertainty a local anchor. Isabel's precise first-marriage timing remains unresolved in the checked evidence and is not used to determine the plot.
- **Religious knighthood already exists.** Gilbert imitates and distorts an existing ideal; he no longer anticipates its orthodox formation. Robert remains a secular knight with household responsibilities.
- **Care is English and local.** A fictional charitable household works beside established religious neighbors. The old Antonine shrine-medical package is not transplanted.
- **The population is not uniformly ruined or hostile.** Particular coercion and a compromised supply route create the local crisis while work, worship, kindness, and legitimate authority remain visible.
- **Names and biographies are rebuilt together.** The four story documents use the new cast and generation. Historical people retain their actual names and dated roles.
- **Christ answers the exact prayer.** The outcome is restored hope, will, conviction, goodness recognized, and meaningful victory, not a numerical worthiness test.

## Using the evidence

**Documented history** establishes a supported event or institution. **Retrospective account** retains a writer's date, perspective, and accusations. **Story inference** identifies a reconstruction or invention. **Unverified** means that this bounded review does not establish the particular claim; it is not proof that the thing never existed.

The core checks concern the new chronology and story's dependencies. This dossier is not an exhaustive census of every 1149 lord, house, crop, garment, or building. Exact local offices, demands, historical hospital arrangements, remedies, full architectural phases, weather, and sky events retain their stated limits.
