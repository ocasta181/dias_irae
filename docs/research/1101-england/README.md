## How to use the comparison

**Documented difference** means that the report supplies English evidence and a stated comparison with the campaign region. **Shared practice** identifies a feature for which a national contrast would mislead. **Later evidence** retains its later date. **Allegation or legend** identifies a source's claim without treating the claimed event as proven. **Story inference** proposes an authorial use beyond a surviving local record.

An English manuscript or object can provide a better surviving reference without proving that the practice was uniquely English. A family can exist before its later earldom or coat of arms. A Christian saint cult and an alleged secret religion are different subjects. The reports preserve these distinctions.

The [research record](research-notes.md) lists scope decisions, assignments, source review, and validation.

The original six topic reports were researched by separate subagents and reviewed by the parent. A further subagent investigated the town settings. The named households and institutions are dated to the story season; unsupported identities, later developments and unresolved local detail remain marked in the reports.
